# AI Visibility Audit

[Back to the skill catalogue](../README.md)

**Category:** SEO research & audits

## Purpose

Assess how a brand appears in consumer AI discovery answers.

## Main procedure

1. Review brand and audience context.
2. agree discovery questions.
3. collect live platform responses.
4. compare mentions and citations.

## Expected output

An evidence-backed visibility workbook and benchmark summary across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and Perplexity.
