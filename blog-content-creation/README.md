# Blog Content Creation

[Back to the skill catalogue](../README.md)

**Category:** Content creation & quality

## Purpose

Develop researched blog posts and supporting pages aligned with a brand.

## Main procedure

1. Select topics.
2. research search intent and competitors.
3. draft and review copy.
4. align formatting and internal links.
5. prepare delivery.

## Expected output

Reviewed blog or supporting-page drafts, Google Docs, and tracking or handover materials.
